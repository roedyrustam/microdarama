VIDEO STYLE & FORMAT
30-second scene in the style of a modern Indonesian short drama. ONE continuous moment — a
single midday delivery to one house, NOT a trailer, NOT a montage — covered like a real
film: cut between MULTIPLE camera positions, angles changing every few seconds while the
moment flows continuously. Quiet, patient pacing; long stillness is intended. Naturalistic,
underplayed acting. Look: warm-neutral filmic colour grading, fine film grain, shallow
depth of field, accurate Indonesian skin tones, bright midday light that never blows out.
This is a quiet family drama, NOT horror and NOT a thriller. NOT sinetron, NO dramatic
zooms, NO jump scares, NO rapid cutting.

LANGUAGE
All spoken words in INDONESIAN (Bahasa Indonesia), Jakarta register, casual spoken form —
not formal news-reader Indonesian. Speak the lines exactly as written in the breakdown
below: do not translate them, do not paraphrase them, do not invent extra lines. Exactly
TWO lines are spoken in the whole scene: the phone-app voice once in SHOT 1, and Adit once
in SHOT 5. In every other shot nobody speaks, and Adit's mouth stays closed — no
muttering, no repeating his line early. No subtitles, no captions, no on-screen text of
any kind burned into the frame. Character names are never spoken aloud unless they appear
inside the quotation marks.

OPTICS
Base 35mm spherical at T2.0 — natural perspective, SHALLOW depth of field. Close-ups step
up to 85mm (FOV approx 24 degrees) to isolate Adit from what is behind him. The opening
floor shot and the final view through the gap use a 50mm. Per-shot lens is specified in
the breakdown. No wide-angle distortion, no fisheye, no zoom moves.

CAMERA
Every cut moves to a DIFFERENT physical position. SHOTS 1 to 6 are all OUTSIDE the house;
SHOT 7 is the only view of the inside, and it is seen through the gap under the door.
SHOT 4 is a REVERSE ANGLE: the camera stands at the sliding door, outside, and looks OUT
across the terrace toward the gate and the lane. The other positions: low over the
terrace floor, far back across the lane, flat against the wall beside the door, at the
left end of the terrace, out in the lane through the fence bars, and at floor level at
the gap under the door. Never two consecutive shots from the same position. Each shot
begins with a location tag — follow it. Locked-off tripod shots, with only two slow
pushes; no handheld shake, no orbit, no crane. No slow-motion.

LIGHTING
Hard midday sun almost directly overhead, undiffused — short black shadows straight under
every object, the terrazzo dust glowing white where the sun hits it. The terrace roof
overhang throws a hard-edged band of shade across the sliding door; inside that band the
light turns cool and soft, bounced up from the sunlit lane. In the reverse angle, the
sunlit lane behind Adit is brighter than his face, which sits in the shade of the
overhang. Daylight 5600K throughout; the only strong colour note is the faded green of
Adit's jacket against grey stone. High contrast, but faces in shade keep their detail —
never crushed to black. In the final view through the gap, the room inside is lit only by
milky diffused daylight coming through the frosted glass, soft and directionless.

CRITICAL
ONE character only: Adit, exactly one of him. Nobody else appears at any point — the
lane, the terrace and the room inside are empty of people. Adit is OUTSIDE the house in
every shot; he never enters it. Adit keeps the exact face, hairstyle and clothing from his
reference image in every shot. No face swapping, no morphing, no duplicated people.
Exactly ONE scooter and exactly ONE package in the whole scene — never duplicated, never
vanishing between cuts (see CONTINUITY). Every shot contains visible movement — a person,
a hand, the camera, a leaf, or drifting dust; no frozen still frames. Accurate Indonesian
lip-sync on his one line. His phone screen is always angled away from camera — no
readable screen.

CONTINUITY

  in front of the gate, on the far side of the fence from the house. The fence and the

AUDIO

down on stone. The phone-app voice is flat and synthetic, coming from Adit's chest pouch,
in SHOT 1 only. NO musical score at all. Adit's one line spoken quietly, almost to
himself, in SHOT 5 only.

MOVEMENT NOTE
Adit is still. He stands with his weight on one leg and his thumb on his phone, and he
does not shift, pace or look around. Every movement he makes is small and finished: one
press of the bell, then a second; the box set down exactly and squared with both hands.
He never sits — when he goes down, he crouches.
